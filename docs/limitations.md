# Limitations and provenance

The repository contains local annual CSV snapshots but no verified source URL, retrieval time, market-definition guide, price unit, or update cadence. Those fields must remain unknown until independently documented.

The filename `2026.csv` does not establish full-year 2026 coverage. All reporting uses parsed `Arrival_Date`; cross-year rows and exact 2025/2026 overlaps are explicitly audited. Results are descriptive, not causal. Potential anomalies are not proof of market manipulation, fraud, or an input error. Forecast intervals and accuracy claims are intentionally not made by the baseline-only implementation.
